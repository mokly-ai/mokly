## Every workspace is on a plan

A new workspace is on the free plan, which publishes open-source
catalogues: every project is public, and projects and seats are unlimited.
Team adds private projects to the same workspace, billed monthly for the
people who use it.

## A seat follows a role

A seat comes from the role a person already holds, so there is one thing to
decide rather than two.

| Seat         | Who holds it               | Monthly price |
| ------------ | -------------------------- | ------------- |
| Editor       | Owners, admins and members | $20           |
| Collaborator | Reviewers                  | $5            |

A project grant counts the same way: admin and member are Editors, reviewer is
a Collaborator. Someone who holds several roles across the workspace and its
projects takes one seat, the stronger of the two kinds. Seats count the people
who have joined, so an invitation starts counting when it is accepted.

Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax.

## Team starts with a trial

Team begins with a 7-day trial, offered once per workspace. Inviting a
member or granting project access while on Team names the seat it adds and its
monthly price beside the control, so the bill is visible where it changes.

## Upgrade from the billing pane

Settings holds a billing pane for each workspace. An owner upgrades there
and returns there to change the payment method, read the renewal date or end
Team. Owners and admins read the plan, the seats in use and the monthly total;
members and reviewers see the plan name on the workspace pane and can ask an
owner to upgrade.

## When Team ends

Private projects stay private, and the publications already in them stay
readable to the people who could read them. Publishing a new catalogue to a
private project takes Team again, and public projects carry on unchanged.
